An explorer needed detailed magnetics over a 10 square kilometre gold project
in the Murchison to refine drill targeting. Regional government coverage existed but
at a line spacing that smoothed over the structural detail that mattered.

**What we flew.** Traverse lines at 25 m spacing with tie lines, QuSpin QTFM Gen-2
in towed-bird configuration at a sensor height of 25 m above ground, terrain-draped,
with a proton precession base station recording on site for the duration of the
survey.

**Processing.** Diurnal correction against the on-site base, heading error correction,
IGRF removal, tie-line levelling and microlevelling, then RTP and first vertical
derivative grids for interpretation. Every step documented in the survey report.

**Deliverables.** Geosoft GRD grids, GeoTIFFs and georeferenced PDF maps in GDA2020,
with line data and a data dictionary. Delivered ready to load into the client's
interpretation workflow.
